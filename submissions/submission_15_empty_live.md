## 💡 3. What I Learned

1. **Deploy Early Milestone:** Shipping an empty but live page eliminates deployment setup friction before populating case study content.
2. **Device Verification:** Verifying layouts on mobile devices catches viewport overflow and typography scaling issues early.
3. **Context Consolidation:** Loading all design tokens, case studies, and content maps into standing instructions allows AI assistants to build cohesive UI components on demand.
