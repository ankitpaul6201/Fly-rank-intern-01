## ✂️ 3. Discernment & Rejection Log (Why AI Assets Were Cut)

### Rejection Case 1: Futuristic 3D Glowing AI Cyber-Brain Illustration
* **What was generated:** A glowing blue 3D neural network brain with neon particles intended for the Hero background.
* **Why it was rejected:** It looked like generic stock marketing fluff that directly contradicted the voice card (`sharp, empirical, plain, direct, evidence-first`). It competed with the proof statement and made the portfolio look like a crypto template.
* **Replacement:** Replaced with a clean, vector workflow transformation diagram built with identity kit hex codes (`#0F172A`, `#2563EB`).

### Rejection Case 2: Midjourney Synthetic Dashboard Mockup
* **What was generated:** A polished, glossy AI-generated SaaS analytics dashboard with synthetic charts for the FlyRank case study.
* **Why it was rejected:** Synthetic dashboards invite skepticism from senior data science leads. A hiring manager evaluating applied ML models wants to see real pandas terminal logs, data contract assertions, and client-holdout metrics, not fake UI concepts.
* **Replacement:** Replaced with real matplotlib Precision@50 benchmark outputs and actual Python terminal logs generated from `work/notebooks/w02_ml_task_framing.ipynb`.

## 💡 4. What I Learned

1. **Proof Over Polish:** Real terminal logs and benchmark charts carry far more credibility than synthetic AI illustrations.
2. **Connective Tissue Consistency:** Visual elements (icons, diagrams) must inherit the exact identity kit palette (`#F8FAFC`, `#2563EB`) to feel like an intentional design system.
3. **Ruthless Discernment:** AI makes generation effortless, so engineering judgment is demonstrated by what you choose to cut.
